## Decide the Buyer Channel Before the Launch

International exposure is usually treated as decoration added after a listing is already public. For a Kendall single-family home that is backwards. Kendall is not one market. The difference between neighborhoods a few streets apart is real, and buyers know it. If Spain, LATAM, relocation, or investor buyers form part of the likely demand pool, the buyer narrative and the distribution plan should exist before the property goes live.

## What a Cross-Border Buyer Verifies in Kendall

A buyer reviewing from abroad cannot walk the property on a weekend. They rely on documentation, and here that means:

- Permit history for additions, enclosed spaces, and in-law conversions
- Association rules where the home sits inside one
- Roof, HVAC, and plumbing age

Incomplete documents do not merely slow the process — they get the property skipped in favor of one that answers the question immediately. The common casualty is unpermitted enclosed space counted as living area in the listing and removed by the appraiser.

## How Kendall Value Reads From Abroad

A buyer who cannot visit prices what they can verify: the specific neighborhood rather than the wider area, lot size and genuinely usable outdoor space, and whether additions were permitted and count as living area. Multigenerational buyers place real value on space that other buyers discount. How the floor plan is described changes which of them shows up. The listing has to make that case in documents and media, because nobody is walking the block on the buyer's behalf.
