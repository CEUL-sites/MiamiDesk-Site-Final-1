With Freddie Mac's 30-year fixed mortgage averaging 7.28% on November 19, 2026, monthly payment structure—not list price alone—is deciding residential contracts across Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach. Major homebuilders are subsidizing quick move-in inventory through captive-lender 2-1 and permanent rate buydowns plus flex-cash credits, pulling payment-sensitive buyers away from unadjusted resale listings. Yet resale homeowners hold a direct counter-move: under Fannie Mae Selling Guide B3-4.1-02 (up to 6% concessions at 80% LTV) and FHA's flat 6% cap, a resale seller can fund a 2-1 rate buydown on an illustrative $700,000 home for roughly $13,218 (1.89%), lowering a buyer's Year 1 principal-and-interest payment by $729 per month—over six times the $109 monthly relief of a $20,000 asking price cut—while protecting neighborhood comparable sales. For buyers, comparing new construction against resale requires auditing captive-lender fees, CDD assessments, tax resets, and Florida's December 31, 2026 Amendment 3 residency timeline before signing.

## Fannie Mae B3-4.1-02 and FHA Seller Concession Caps

Both developer incentives and resale seller concessions must stay within federal agency Interested Party Contribution (IPC) rules:

| Loan Program & Occupancy Tier | LTV / Down Payment Band | Maximum Seller / Builder Concession (IPC Cap) |
|---|---|---|
| **Fannie Mae Conventional (Primary / 2nd Home)** | >90% LTV (<10% down) | **3%** |
| **Fannie Mae Conventional (Primary / 2nd Home)** | 75.01% – 90% LTV (10%–24.99% down) | **6%** |
| **Fannie Mae Conventional (Primary / 2nd Home)** | <=75% LTV (25%+ down) | **9%** |
| **Fannie Mae Conventional (Investment Property)** | All LTVs | **2%** |
| **FHA Insured Mortgage** | Standard (3.5%+ down) | **6%** flat cap |

Under Fannie Mae B3-4.1-02, temporary interest rate buydowns count inside these same maximum concession tiers. Concessions may cover closing costs, prepaid items, discount points, and buydown escrow funds, but **cannot** fund the borrower's minimum down payment or exceed total actual closing and buydown costs. Any excess above agency ceilings is treated as a sales concession and deducted dollar-for-dollar from the purchase price before LTV calculation.

## Four Hidden Variables Buyers Must Audit Before Choosing New Construction

Builders across Palm Beach, western Broward, and suburban Miami-Dade are heavily promoting rate buydowns and flex-cash packages—especially on quick move-in (spec) inventory—while elevated construction-material costs (~10% higher year-over-year) and labor costs tighten builder margins. Buyers comparing [South Florida new construction](/new-construction?utm_source=journal&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=south-florida-new-construction-vs-resale-rate-buydowns-2026) against resale homes should verify four critical factors before signing a builder contract:

1. **Captive-Lender Fee Trade-Offs:** Aggressive builder rate buydowns almost always require using the builder's affiliated mortgage company. Obtain a parallel Loan Estimate from an independent lender to confirm higher origination points or base prices are not quietly absorbing the advertised rate discount.
2. **CDD Assessments, HOA Dues & Improved-Value Tax Resets:** Many master-planned communities in Broward and Palm Beach carry Community Development District (CDD) bond assessments alongside HOA fees. Furthermore, first-year closing tax estimates based on unimproved land jump sharply once the county Property Appraiser assesses the finished home.
3. **Delivery Timeline & December 31, 2026 Amendment 3 Residency Cutoff:** Following voter approval of Florida Amendment 3 on November 3, 2026, homeowners who establish permanent Florida residency and hold title on or before **December 31, 2026** (filing Form DR-501 by March 1, 2027) qualify for the accelerated non-school homestead exemption phase-in ($150,000 in 2027 and $250,000 in 2028). Ground-up builds closing in mid-2027 place new Florida residents into a five-year waiting period before enhanced tiers apply, whereas quick move-in spec inventory or resale homes closing before year-end can preserve residency timing.
4. **Established Coastal Land Scarcity:** South Florida's physical boundary between the Atlantic Ocean and the Everglades limits mature residential infill in [Weston](/sell-weston), [Coral Gables](/sell-coral-gables), [Doral](/sell-doral), [Fort Lauderdale](/sell-fort-lauderdale), and [Brickell](/sell-brickell)—the long-run structural driver reflected in 50-year FHFA constant-quality data.
