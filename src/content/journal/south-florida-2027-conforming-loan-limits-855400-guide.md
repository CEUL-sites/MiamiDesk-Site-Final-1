On November 24, 2026, the Federal Housing Finance Agency (FHFA) raised the 2027 baseline conforming loan limit for one-unit properties across **Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach** counties to **$855,400**, effective January 1, 2027. That **$22,650 (+2.72%)** increase from 2026's $832,750 ceiling lifts maximum conforming purchase prices to **$900,421** with 5% down, **$950,444** with 10% down, and **$1,069,250** with 20% down. Multi-unit limits also rise to **$1,095,250** (duplex), **$1,323,900** (triplex), and **$1,645,350** (fourplex). With Freddie Mac's 30-year fixed average at **7.28%** (November 19, 2026), keeping loans at or below $855,400 helps buyers avoid Jumbo reserve overlays while giving $900,000-to-$1,140,000 sellers a wider qualified buyer pool—provided condominiums pass agency warrantability rules.

## 2027 Conforming Loan Limits Across South Florida (1 to 4 Units)

Under the Housing and Economic Recovery Act (HERA), annual limits track the nominal, seasonally adjusted U.S. FHFA House Price Index (+2.72% from Q3 2025 to Q3 2026). While Monroe County in the Florida Keys carries a $1,035,000 one-unit limit (national high-cost ceiling: $1,283,100), Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach share the standard national baseline:

## Four Financing and Seller Rules Deciding Contracts Near $855,400

1. **Conforming vs. Jumbo Reserve Overlays:** While Jumbo 30-year fixed rates often price within 0.20% to 0.25% of conforming averages, Jumbo underwriting typically mandates **20% down** plus **6 to 12 months of verified liquid PITI reserves** on loans up to $2.5 million. Primary-residence conforming files allow 5% to 20% down with minimal post-closing reserve requirements, preserving liquidity for insurance escrows and closing costs.
2. **Warrantable Condos vs. Non-Warrantable Inventory:** Condominiums and townhomes only qualify for the $855,400 limit when the association clears Fannie Mae Full Review (Form 1076) insurance, reserve, and Milestone Inspection / Structural Integrity Reserve Study (SIRS) rules ahead of Florida's December 31, 2026 statutory cutoff. See our [2026 South Florida condo warrantability briefing](/journal/south-florida-condo-warrantability-fannie-mae-full-review-2026) for project-level criteria.
3. **Timing January 2027 Delivery and Early Lender Locks:** The $855,400 ceiling applies to loans delivered to Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac starting January 1, 2027. Many South Florida lenders accept applications immediately using 2027 limits for December contracts closing in early January; confirm exact pipeline cutoffs with a licensed loan officer.
4. **Pricing Strategy for $900,000 to $1,140,000 Sellers:** Homeowners listing near $955,000 or $1,085,000 risk pushing 10% to 20% down buyers across the $855,400 Jumbo boundary. Strategic pricing—or pairing a conforming-aligned price with a seller-funded rate buydown inside Fannie Mae B3-4.1-02 limits (detailed in our [2026 rate buydown and seller concession analysis](/journal/south-florida-new-construction-vs-resale-rate-buydowns-2026))—keeps financed buyers qualified alongside cash offers.
