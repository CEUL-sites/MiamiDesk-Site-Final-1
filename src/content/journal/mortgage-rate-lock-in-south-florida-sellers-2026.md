The decision to sell has two sides: the property you are leaving and the home, investment or relocation you are funding next. A favorable sale price can still produce an uncomfortable move if replacement costs are not modeled first.

Freddie Mac reported a 30-year fixed mortgage average of **7.18%** and a 15-year average of **6.57%** for November 5, 2026. Those are dated national survey averages, not a personal lending quote. [Source: Freddie Mac PMMS](https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms)

> A low existing mortgage rate is an asset in the household budget. The selling decision also depends on available equity, replacement costs and the purpose of the move.

## The 85.3% payment gap needs its own assumptions

Realtor.com’s November 11 analysis estimated that a typical mortgage holder’s monthly principal and interest would rise from **$1,332 to $2,468**, an **85.3% increase**, when buying a median-priced home. Its scenario used a **7.32% mortgage rate**, a **$419,250 September 2026 median listing price** and a **14.3% down payment**. It did not use the newer 7.18% Freddie Mac average. [Source: Realtor.com](https://mediaroom.realtor.com/2026-11-11-Buying-a-Median-Priced-Home-Would-Cost-Typical-Mortgage-Holders-About-1%2C100-More-a-Month)

This comparison includes both different financing terms and a different home purchase. It is not the effect of changing the interest rate alone. Principal and interest also leave out taxes, insurance, association charges, maintenance and moving expenses. Your replacement-home budget should account for those items separately.

## Miami’s long-run price history provides context

FHFA All-Transactions HPI series, distributed by the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, allow a common-period comparison. Rebase each series to its own 1975 Q4 observation rather than comparing raw index levels with different original bases.

- **[Miami–Miami Beach–Kendall metropolitan division](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/ATNHPIUS33124Q):** 36.87 → 666.21; 18.07×; +1,706.92%.

- **[Florida](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/FLSTHPI):** 68.14 → 825.70; 12.12×; +1,111.77%.

- **[United States](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/USSTHPI):** 62.31 → 719.87; 11.55×; +1,055.30%.

Author calculations: ending index divided by starting index; cumulative change equals that ratio minus one, multiplied by 100. The period is 1975 Q4–2026 Q2, approximately 50.5 years. Original bases differ: Miami uses 1995 Q1 = 100; Florida and the U.S. use 1980 Q1 = 100. Figures are nominal index changes, not annualized returns, rental yields or net investment returns.

FHFA uses repeat mortgage transactions on single-family properties financed through Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac; the All-Transactions series includes refinance appraisal information. It does not represent every cash sale, luxury property or condominium. [Source: FHFA methodology](https://www.fhfa.gov/faqs/hpi)

The Miami series covers the Miami–Miami Beach–Kendall metropolitan division. It is not a Weston, Broward or all-South-Florida index. Its historical growth does not establish that South Florida is insulated from mortgage rates or that an individual property will appreciate. That is the limit of the evidence.

## Review three decisions before setting a listing date

### 1. Establish the cash available after selling

Separate estimated market value from usable proceeds. Account for mortgage payoff, negotiated selling expenses, concessions, repairs and any tax obligations your adviser identifies. Start with a [property-value review](/home-value), then develop a property-specific net-proceeds estimate.

### 2. Price the next move using current carrying costs

Compare at least two replacement scenarios: financing the next home and using more sale proceeds to reduce borrowing. Where relevant, evaluate a smaller cash purchase. Include insurance, taxes, association costs and reserves. A lender and qualified tax adviser should review financing and tax assumptions.

If timing is the obstacle, our [downsizing and post-occupancy guide](/journal/weston-downsizing-equity-post-occupancy-homestead-portability) explains planning questions to discuss. Any post-closing occupancy depends on negotiated terms and the transaction’s financing and insurance requirements.

### 3. Test the property against its actual competition

Before choosing a price, examine recent comparable closings, competing active listings, condition, assessment obligations and the likely buyer’s financing constraints. Cash availability, supply and relocation demand should be tested in the relevant price band and property category. A regional narrative cannot replace that work.

## Questions owners ask

### Does a 7.18% national mortgage average determine my home’s value?

No. A national mortgage average is financing context. Property-specific pricing requires current competing listings, recent comparable sales, condition and transaction terms.

### Does Miami’s historical price growth show that mortgage rates do not matter?

No. A long-run house-price index does not measure current rate sensitivity or establish the value of a particular property.
