Beginning **February 1, 2027**, Florida Statutes § 689.261 (amended via HB 7031E) prohibits online residential listing platforms from using a current seller's Save Our Homes-capped property tax bill in payment calculators. Portals must estimate prospective taxes from the listing price and county millage data—or link directly to the County Property Appraiser—reshaping buyer underwriting across Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach.

## What Changes on February 1, 2027 Under Fla. Stat. § 689.261

Public portals historically displayed the seller's tax bill beside an active listing. Because Save Our Homes caps annual homestead assessment increases at 3% (or CPI, if lower), long-term owners pay taxes far below current market value. Upon sale, the County Property Appraiser reassesses the parcel at market value the following January 1.

Championed by the Property Appraisers' Association of Florida (PAAF) via HB 7031E, the updated statute changes online displays:

| Statutory Rule | Prior Portal Practice | Starting Feb 1, 2027 (Fla. Stat. § 689.261) |
|---|---|---|
| **Covered Platforms** | Optional historical display | Websites, web apps, and mobile apps (excluding social media) |
| **Tax Calculator Basis** | Seller's capped tax bill | **Prohibited** from using current owner's assessment or taxes |
| **Approved Formula** | Portal defaults | Listing price + FL Dept. of Revenue / county aggregate millage |
| **Alternative Path** | Historical bill + fine print | Omit estimate and link to County Property Appraiser estimator |
| **Contract Disclosure** | Property Tax Summary | Mandatory statutory disclosure remains required at signing |

## Amendment 3, Closing Costs, and Carrying-Cost Alignment

Evaluate the February 1, 2027 listing rule alongside two related 2027 shifts:

1. **Florida Amendment 3 Residency Timing:** Voters expanded the non-school homestead exemption to **up to $150,000** for 2027 (and $250,000 in 2028; school taxes keep the $25,000 exemption). Permanent Florida residency by **December 31, 2026** qualifies immediately for 2027; buyers arriving on or after January 1, 2027 receive the $50,000 baseline for four years. See our [Florida Amendment 3 seller portability guide](/journal/florida-amendment-3-home-sellers-homestead-portability-2026).
2. **Combined Escrow Pressure:** Tax estimates sit beside hazard and flood insurance in DTI underwriting. Single-family Citizens policyholders should review our [Florida Citizens 2027 flood insurance mandate guide](/journal/florida-citizens-2027-flood-insurance-mandate-south-florida-sellers).
3. **County Transfer Taxes:** Deed documentary stamps remain $0.70 per $100 in Broward and Palm Beach, while Miami-Dade charges $0.60 per $100 on single-family deeds plus a $0.45 surtax on non-single-family transfers. Model net proceeds in our [South Florida seller closing costs guide](/journal/seller-closing-costs-south-florida-2026).

## Three Steps for South Florida Sellers Preparing for 2027

1. **Run Official County Tax Estimates Before Launch:** Portal formulas use countywide aggregate millage rates that can diverge from municipal levies. Prepare an official County Property Appraiser estimate—plus a Save Our Homes portability worksheet—before listing in [Miami](/sell-miami), [Coral Gables](/sell-coral-gables), [Weston](/sell-weston), or [Fort Lauderdale](/sell-fort-lauderdale).
2. **Pair Pricing With Verified Carrying Costs:** When portals display higher prospective taxes on February 1, 2027, overpriced homes sit longer. Supplying verified 4-Point, wind-mitigation, and municipal tax records upfront prevents mid-contract renegotiation.
3. **Sequence Your Replacement Purchase:** Long-term homesteaded sellers transferring Save Our Homes equity protect annual tax liability when closing dates and portability filings are coordinated with a CPA or real estate attorney.
