## South Florida's Cash-Led Split Market Entering 2027

With Freddie Mac's 30-year fixed mortgage average at **7.21%** on November 25, 2026 (15-year fixed at **6.54%**), domestic leveraged demand has cooled while all-cash capital sets the clearing price. According to MIAMI REALTORS® October 2026 county statistics, all-cash transactions accounted for **37.9%** of Miami-Dade closings, **38.8%** in Broward, and **51.7%** in Palm Beach, while distressed sales remained minimal at **0.4% to 1.3%**:

For leveraged cross-border buyers qualifying for U.S. mortgages, FHFA's **2027** 1-unit conforming loan limit rises to **$855,400** on January 1, 2027, lifting a 20%-down purchase ceiling to **$1,069,250** for single-family homes and warrantable condominiums.

## Bilateral Tax and Regulatory Matrix: South Florida vs. Spain (2027)

Cross-border investors comparing capital deployment between South Florida and prime Spanish corridors such as Madrid or Marbella and the Costa del Sol face distinct statutory thresholds on entry, holding, and exit:

| Rule or Metric (2026–2027) | South Florida (Florida / U.S.) | Spain (Madrid & Andalucía) | Planning Implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Residency Linked to Purchase** | Property purchase confers no U.S. visa | Golden Visa (€500,000 route) repealed Apr 3, 2025 (`Ley Orgánica 1/2025`) | Acquisitions must stand on yield, liquidity, and estate goals |
| **Buyer Closing / Transfer Tax** | Documentary stamps & title costs; no state income tax | Resale ITP: **6%** Madrid / **7%** Andalucía (new build: 10% IVA + AJD) | Spain entry tax base uses higher of deed price or *valor de referencia* |
| **Annual Property Tax Cap** | **5%** non-homestead cap starts 2027 (Amendment 3); $150K homestead tier if resident by Dec 31, 2026 | IBI municipal tax + **24%** non-EU IRNR tax on **gross** rent (no deductions) | U.S. owners in Spain pay 24% gross vs. 19% net for EU/EEA owners |
| **Exit & Wealth Withholding** | **FIRPTA**: 15% default gross withholding (> $1M or investment); Form 8288-B reduces excess | €700K non-resident wealth allowance; >€3M net assets hit **1.7%–3.5%** Solidarity Tax | Both jurisdictions require pre-listing CPA and legal structuring |

Two dates govern late-2026 cross-border decisions:

1. **Florida Amendment 3 (December 31, 2026 Residency Cutoff):** Starting in 2027, Amendment 3 cuts the annual assessment cap for non-homestead properties (second homes and rentals) from 10% to **5%** for non-school taxes—detailed in our [Amendment 3 investor tax analysis](/journal/florida-amendment-3-property-tax-investors-landlords-2027). Meanwhile, primary-residence buyers who establish permanent Florida residency by **December 31, 2026** unlock the **$150,000** non-school homestead exemption in 2027 ($250,000 in 2028) instead of waiting five years at the baseline ~$50,000 tier.
2. **FIRPTA Pre-Listing Readiness & Spain Modelo 210 Deadlines:** Non-U.S. sellers face **15%** gross withholding under FIRPTA (10% on $300,001–$1,000,000 or 0% at $300,000 or below only when a buyer signs a two-year personal-residence test). As outlined in our [FIRPTA guide for foreign sellers](/journal/firpta-withholding-foreign-seller-south-florida-2026), filing IRS Form 8288-B before closing avoids tying up equity in refund delays. In Spain, Order HAC/623/2026 moved non-resident annual Modelo 210 rental filings to **April 1–20** (first governing 2026 rental receipts filed in April 2027)—covered in our [U.S. investor guide to Spain](/journal/spain-american-investors-2026).
