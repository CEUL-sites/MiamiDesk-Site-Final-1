## Why Warrantability Governs Condo Liquidity Across the Tri-County Corridor

Fresh October 2026 MIAMI REALTORS® and RWorld figures show how financing friction reshapes condominium trading across all three counties:

- **Miami-Dade County:** Condo median sale prices stood at **$397,500** (-0.6% year-over-year) across **12.7 months** of active supply and roughly 70 median days to contract. Nearly **50%** of all condo closings—and **82%** of $1 million-plus Miami condo transactions—settle in cash.
- **Broward County:** Condo medians adjusted 7.2% year-over-year to **$240,300** across **$279 million** in monthly condo volume (-15.95% year-over-year), yet distressed transactions sit at a historic low of **1.2%**, signaling patient owner equity rather than forced sales.
- **Palm Beach County:** Condo medians settled at **$300,000** (-4.8% year-over-year) while single-family medians climbed 6.1% to $682,500.
- **Financing & Insurance Carrying Costs:** With Freddie Mac's 30-year fixed mortgage averaging **7.28%** on November 19, 2026, financed condo buyers face tighter debt-to-income ratios. One positive offset entering winter: OIR-approved 2026 Citizens Property Insurance rate reductions average **-10.4% for HO-6 condo unit-owner policies** (-8.7% for HO-3 homeowners), reducing unit-level carrying friction when buildings maintain sound master insurance.

## What Fannie Mae Full Review and December 31, 2026 SIRS Rules Actually Check

Under Fannie Mae's updated underwriting standards, higher down payments no longer bypass building scrutiny on projects above 10 units. Lenders evaluate five decisive criteria through Form 1076/1076A condo questionnaires and association financials:

1. **Completed Milestone Inspection & SIRS (`Fla. Stat. § 553.899` & `§ 718.112`):** Buildings of three or more habitable stories turning 30 years old (or 25 years in qualifying coastal jurisdictions) require Milestone Inspections and non-waivable SIRS funding for core structural components. Associations coordinating SIRS with a milestone inspection face an outer completion cutoff of **December 31, 2026**. Under **HB 913**, the statutory structural reserve-item cost threshold increased from $10,000 to **$25,000** (subject to DBPR inflation adjustments), and unit-owner-controlled associations may vote by majority to pause or reduce reserve contributions for up to **two consecutive annual budgets** immediately after a milestone inspection to prioritize urgent structural repairs.
2. **No Unfunded Critical Repairs or Safety Violations:** Ongoing concrete spalling, deferred structural work, or unresolved local recertification notices trigger immediate Fannie Mae ineligibility, shifting a building into a cash or non-warrantable lending pool.
3. **Reserve Budget & Delinquency Ratios:** Full Review verifies dedicated annual replacement reserve allocations and checks that unit-owner HOA assessment delinquencies remain within agency caps (typically 15%).
4. **Master Insurance & Litigation:** Adequate master property and windstorm coverage and the absence of material structural litigation remain mandatory.
5. **Narrow Project Waivers:** Waivers bypassing Full Review apply strictly to detached site condos, small projects of 10 or fewer units without critical repairs, and select internal Fannie Mae refinances.

## Action Playbook for South Florida Condo Buyers and Sellers

### For Condominium Buyers: Pre-Offer Building Vetting

Never wait until day 25 of a financing contingency to discover a building is non-warrantable. Before submitting an offer in [Brickell](/sell-brickell), [Aventura](/sell-aventura), [Downtown Miami](/sell-downtown-miami), [Fort Lauderdale](/sell-fort-lauderdale), [Hallandale Beach](/sell-hallandale-beach), or [Pompano Beach](/sell-pompano-beach), request the completed Phase 1 and Phase 2 Milestone Inspection reports, current SIRS study, 2026–2027 adopted budget, master insurance declaration page, and recent board minutes—and run a pre-offer Fannie Mae Form 1076 check with a local condo-experienced lender. In non-warrantable buildings, cash buyers and portfolio-loan buyers hold measurable negotiating leverage on price and assessment credits because conventional borrowers are sidelined.

### For Condominium Sellers: Warrantable Documentation vs. Global Cash Positioning

If your building satisfies Full Review, assemble the completed SIRS, Milestone Inspection, budget, and master insurance certificate before going live so buyers and lenders verify eligibility immediately. If your building is non-warrantable or mid-repair, pricing and distribution must target domestic and international all-cash capital—anchored by South Florida's ocean-to-Everglades coastal land constraints and zero state individual income tax—rather than relying on leveraged buyers whose loans will fail underwriting.
