Positioning a Brickell condominium before pricing it: the building underwriting, not just the unit, and what buyers verify first.

## What Brickell Buyers Are Actually Comparing

Brickell trades like a securities market: same building, same line, different numbers depending on who is selling and why.

Investor buyers price the unit off what it can carry. Owner-occupants price it off how it lives. A listing that addresses only one of those is competing for half the market. The likely buyer here is drawn from financial-district, international, and investor buyers, and the property is being read against a dense high-rise market where the buyer is underwriting the building as much as the unit. A list price set without knowing which comparison the property will lose is a guess wearing a number.

## The Diligence That Decides a Brickell Deal

The items that most often stall a sale here:

- Association financials, reserves, and assessment history
- Structural inspection and reserve funding status
- The building's policy on short-term and seasonal leasing

None of them improve by waiting, and each is cheaper to resolve on the seller's own schedule than under a contract deadline with a buyer holding the calendar. The specific failure to plan around is a lender or insurance carrier taking issue with the building rather than the unit.

## Where the Price Actually Comes From

Pricing should be built from line, floor, and view corridor, finish level relative to the building standard, and parking and whether the unit is currently leased — not from a per-square-foot figure applied across the city. Overpricing does not create negotiating room. It removes the property from the searches of the buyers most likely to pay full value, and the correction costs more than the original discipline would have.

## FAQ

### How long should preparation take on a Brickell condominium?

It depends on what the property needs and what the documents show — here that usually starts with association financials, reserves, and assessment history. The sequence matters more than the calendar: resolve what a buyer will discover, then launch. A listing only goes live for the first time once.

### What does a strategy review actually produce?

A written position: the likely buyer, the pricing range and the evidence behind it, the preparation sequence, and the negotiation posture. It is a working document, not a listing presentation.

### Is there any obligation to list after a review?

No. Plenty of owners use a review to decide the timing is wrong, or that the property should be prepared over a longer horizon. That is a legitimate outcome of the exercise.
