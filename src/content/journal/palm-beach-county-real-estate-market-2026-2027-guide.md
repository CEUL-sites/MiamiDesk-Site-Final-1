In October 2026, **Palm Beach County** led South Florida single-family appreciation at **$682,500** (+6.1% year-over-year) with **3.9 months of supply** and a **45.9% all-cash share**, while condominiums recorded **7.1 months of supply** and a **$300,000** median (-4.8% YoY). Navigating **2027** across Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Jupiter, and West Palm Beach requires separating single-family scarcity from condo warrantability.

## Palm Beach County vs. Miami-Dade and Broward (October 2026)

With Freddie Mac's 30-year fixed average at **7.21%** on November 25, 2026 (down from 7.28% on November 19), cash liquidity decides which South Florida submarkets absorb supply cleanly. Palm Beach County's **45.9% single-family** and **59.7% condo** cash shares helped it post the tightest single-family inventory and the only positive year-over-year condo sales growth (+0.5%) in the tri-county area during October 2026:

## Four Forces Reshaping Palm Beach County Closings in 2027

1. **$855,400 Conforming Loan Ceiling:** Effective January 1, 2027, FHFA raised the Palm Beach County 1-unit conforming limit to **$855,400**, lifting the 20%-down purchase ceiling to **$1,069,250** (see our [2027 conforming loan limits guide](/journal/south-florida-2027-conforming-loan-limits-855400-guide)). That reduces jumbo reserve friction across West Palm Beach, Wellington, Boynton Beach, and Delray Beach.
2. **Condo Reserve Cutoff and Warrantability:** Although Palm Beach County's **7.1 months** condo supply beats Miami-Dade (12.7) and Broward (10.6), only 21 of 2,397 tri-county condo buildings (0.9%) hold active FHA approval. Associations facing the **December 31, 2026** SIRS deadline need clean reserves and Fannie Mae Form 1076 documentation, or sellers must price for the **59.7%** all-cash condo pool covered in our [South Florida condo warrantability guide](/journal/south-florida-condo-warrantability-fannie-mae-full-review-2026).
3. **Citizens Flood Mandate vs. 2026 Rate Relief:** Florida OIR-approved 2026 filings cut Citizens HO-3 renewal rates by an average **11.7%** in Palm Beach County. Starting **January 1, 2027**, remaining Citizens personal wind policies (excluding HO-6 condo policies) require separate flood coverage even in Zone X—details in our [Citizens 2027 flood mandate guide](/journal/florida-citizens-2027-flood-insurance-mandate-south-florida-sellers).
4. **February 1, 2027 Portal Property Tax Rule:** Under amended Florida Statutes § 689.261, portals cannot use a seller's Save Our Homes-capped tax bill in payment calculators starting **February 1, 2027**—see our [Florida 2027 online listing tax law guide](/journal/florida-2027-online-listing-property-tax-estimate-law-south-florida).

## Long-Run Equity Context: FHFA Repeat-Sales Indices

Monthly MLS medians reflect sales mix, whereas constant-quality FHFA repeat-sales indices measure appreciation on identical single-family homes and must never be conflated with monthly median prices. In the annual FHFA county series (**1975–2025**), Palm Beach County (`ATNHPIUS12099A`) compounded **10.48x (+947.73%)**, outperforming Broward (`ATNHPIUS12011A`, **9.77x**, +877.32%) while trailing Miami-Dade (`ATNHPIUS12086A`, **16.76x**, +1,576.06%). Across the 50-year quarterly baseline (**1975 Q4 – 2026 Q2**), Greater Miami (`ATNHPIUS33124Q`) reached **18.07x** (+1,706.92%, 5.90% CAGR) vs. Florida (`FLSTHPI`, **12.12x**, 5.06% CAGR) and the U.S. (`USSTHPI`, **11.55x**, 4.96% CAGR).
