If you are considering a home in Weston, the cash needed to close deserves a closer look before you rule out a property. The [City of Weston's housing assistance page](https://www.westonfl.org/residents/housing-assistance-programs) describes a **Homebuyer Purchase Assistance Program of up to $120,000** for qualified applicants purchasing within the city. The actual amount depends on financial need and program review. It is not an automatic credit or cash grant.

## Who may qualify?

According to the City, a buyer must first qualify for a mortgage that meets Broward County guidelines, must not have owned residential property during the previous three years, and must use the Weston home as a primary residence. The buyer must contribute at least **3% of the purchase price**; the City says **up to 2% may come from a gift**. Other income, property, lender, and program conditions may apply.

The assistance is described as a **0% interest loan**. The City says it is forgiven after 15 years if the buyer continues to own, occupy, and maintain the home under program terms. Confirm what events could trigger repayment with the County before relying on the assistance in an offer.

## What does “up to $120,000” mean in practice?

It is a ceiling, not an amount every applicant receives. A lender and the County need to assess the buyer's financing, household eligibility, eligible property, available funds, and transaction timing. A buyer should not assume that the program can replace the required personal contribution or that the full amount will be available for a particular home.

The purchase budget still needs to account for the mortgage payment, taxes, insurance, association fees, inspections, and closing costs. In Weston, association rules and fees can differ substantially by community. We review those costs alongside the property and contract terms before a buyer makes a commitment.

## A practical first step

1. Speak with a lender who can evaluate your first mortgage against Broward County's program guidelines.
2. Contact the [Broward County Housing and Urban Planning Division](https://www.broward.org/Housing) at **954-357-4943** to confirm current funding, income limits, property rules, application steps, and timing.
3. Once the financing route is clear, compare Weston properties on total monthly cost and condition, then structure an offer that allows for the lender and County review.
