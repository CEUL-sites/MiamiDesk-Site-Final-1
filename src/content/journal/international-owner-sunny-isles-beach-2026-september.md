## Decide the Buyer Channel Before the Launch

International exposure is usually treated as decoration added after a listing is already public. For a Sunny Isles Beach condominium that is backwards. ## What a Cross-Border Buyer Verifies in Sunny Isles Beach

A buyer reviewing from abroad cannot walk the property on a weekend. They rely on documentation, and here that means:

- Association reserves, assessment history, and structural inspection status
- The building's leasing policy
- How oceanfront exposure affects maintenance and insurance on the unit

Incomplete documents do not merely slow the process — they get the property skipped in favor of one that answers the question immediately. The common casualty is a cross-border buyer stalling on documents that were never assembled in advance.
