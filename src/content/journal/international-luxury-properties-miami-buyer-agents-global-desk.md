The **Miami Global Desk** connects international owners, developers and listing agencies with South Florida's professional real estate market.

An exceptional property in Madrid, Marbella, Lisbon, Mexico City, the Caribbean or another international luxury market can have worldwide online visibility and still remain largely invisible to the professionals who influence many cross-border purchases.

That is the problem the **Miami Global Desk** is designed to address.

The objective is not simply to place another property advertisement online. It is to create a professional distribution and cooperation bridge between **international luxury real estate** and the real estate professionals serving buyers and investors throughout Miami and South Florida.

For an international property owner, developer or listing agency, that creates a very different type of exposure.

## Beyond Consumer Portals: Reaching the Professionals Behind the Buyer

Luxury properties are already marketed across websites, social media and international portals.

But a significant number of high-value transactions are influenced by a trusted real estate advisor.

A buyer in Miami may tell an agent:

> "We are considering a second home in Madrid."

An investor may ask about opportunities in Spain.

A Latin American family already established in South Florida may want to acquire a European residence.

A Miami-based advisor may have a client looking for lifestyle, diversification, relocation or investment opportunities outside the United States.

The critical question for the international seller is therefore not simply:

**Can buyers find my property online?**

It is also:

**Can the professionals advising those buyers find it, understand it and confidently introduce it to their clients?**

That is where the Global Desk fits.

## What the Miami Global Desk Does

The Global Desk provides a structured U.S. activation layer for selected international properties, working through **Carlos Uzcategui and United Realty Group** in South Florida.

The international owner, developer or local listing agency maintains control of the underlying property mandate.

The Global Desk adds the South Florida side of the distribution and cooperation infrastructure: professional positioning, brokerage coordination, property information prepared for the U.S. market, buyer-agent visibility, defined cooperation economics where applicable, and a clear process for handling interested buyers and referrals.

The purpose is simple:

**Your local mandate remains local. Your distribution becomes international.**

Where a property qualifies for applicable professional real-estate distribution channels, the objective is to make it easier for South Florida real estate professionals to discover the opportunity and present it to appropriate clients.

It is not direct access to an MLS, nor is it simply a portal subscription. Participation remains subject to applicable brokerage, MLS, property and cooperation requirements.

## Why Miami and South Florida Matter

South Florida represents one of the world's most internationally connected real-estate markets.

Following the 2026 combination of MIAMI REALTORS® and RWorld, the unified organization encompasses approximately **93,000 members**, making it the world's largest local REALTOR® association.

Its international infrastructure extends considerably further. The association reported more than **437 international agreements with real-estate organizations worldwide**, together with **11 MLS data exchanges in the United States and Canada** and continuing development of cross-border data initiatives.

For an international property owner, the significance is not the headline number by itself.

It is the professional ecosystem behind it.

Miami and South Florida real estate professionals advise buyers from the United States, Latin America, Europe and other global markets. Those relationships can become an additional distribution channel for carefully selected international properties.

For owners of luxury residences, second homes and distinctive international properties, Global Desk activation can add another layer to an existing marketing strategy.

Rather than relying exclusively on direct-to-consumer advertising, the property can be positioned for professionals who may already have relationships with the type of buyers the property is trying to reach.

This can be particularly relevant for:

- Luxury residences and second homes

- Investment-oriented opportunities

- Distinctive properties with appeal beyond their local market

The Global Desk does not replace strong local representation. It is designed to **extend the reach of that representation into South Florida's professional buyer market.**

## The Benefit for International Listing Agents and Agencies

The model is equally relevant to the agent or brokerage already representing the property.

The international listing professional keeps the local mandate and market expertise.

The Global Desk provides a South Florida cooperation bridge.

When an interested buyer or buyer representative emerges, the goal is to create an organized professional handoff rather than deliver an unqualified internet inquiry.

Property interest can be reviewed, the professional relationship identified, relevant information clarified and the opportunity transferred to the appropriate listing representative under agreed brokerage and cooperation procedures.

That makes the Global Desk potentially valuable not only as a marketing tool, but also as an **additional capability an agency can present when competing for international or developer mandates**.

A seller can reasonably ask:

> How will you expose my property beyond this market?

Having a defined South Florida activation strategy gives the listing agency another answer.

## From Property Exposure to Professional Cooperation

The Global Desk model can be summarized in four stages:

**International Property → South Florida Activation → Buyer-Agent Interest → Professional Introduction**

The property is first evaluated for suitability.

Its presentation is then adapted for the South Florida professional market, including the information a U.S.-based agent is likely to need to evaluate it for a client.

Interested real estate professionals can obtain information and identify prospective buyer interest.

When appropriate, qualified opportunities are coordinated with the international listing side so that the local professional can continue the transaction with the buyer.

Commission and cooperation arrangements are established separately in writing and handled through the appropriate brokerages when applicable.

This creates a clearer structure for everyone involved: the owner, the international listing professional, the South Florida cooperating professional and the buyer.
