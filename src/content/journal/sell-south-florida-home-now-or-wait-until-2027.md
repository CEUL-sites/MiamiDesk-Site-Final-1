For most South Florida property owners, waiting for spring 2027 exposes a sale to rising seasonal competition without guaranteeing lower borrowing costs. With Freddie Mac's 30-year fixed mortgage rate rebounding to 7.28% on November 19, 2026, and November 18 Federal Reserve minutes confirming a 3.75% to 4.00% federal funds hold, financed buyers remain selective. Meanwhile, newly released October 2026 tri-county market data shows winter inventory already building: Palm Beach single-family listings rose over 20% in a single month, while Miami-Dade condominium supply sits at 12.7 months. Yet South Florida still operates on a cash-driven winter calendar, with nearly half of condominium closings and 82% of Miami $1 million-plus condo sales settling in cash. Listing during the late-2026 winter arrival window captures peak domestic relocation and international liquidity before spring inventory peaks.

## Tri-County Reality: October 2026 Data Across Both Houses and Condos

Fresh MIAMI REALTORS® and RWorld figures for October 2026 highlight why timing decisions must separate single-family residences from condominiums and average price bands from luxury tiers:

- **Miami-Dade County:** Total sales declined 10.6% year-over-year to 1,689 transactions, though 10-month year-to-date volume remains up 4%. Single-family homes posted a **$706,000** median sale price (up 3.5% year-over-year from $682,000) across 5.4 months of supply and roughly 42 median days to contract. Condominiums posted a **$397,500** median (down 0.6% year-over-year from $400,000, dipping below $400,000) across 12.7 months of supply and 70 median days to contract.
- **Broward County:** Total residential dollar volume reached **$1.0 billion** (-9.63% year-over-year), split between $770 million in single-family volume and $279 million in condos. The single-family median held nearly flat at **$610,000** (-0.2% year-over-year from $611,250), while the condo median declined 7.2% to **$240,300** (from $259,000). Crucially, distressed transactions accounted for just **1.2%** of Broward closings, confirming that sellers hold substantial equity without forced-liquidation pressure.
- **Palm Beach County:** Single-family median prices climbed 6.1% year-over-year to **$682,500** (from $643,000), while condo medians adjusted 4.8% to **$300,000** (from $315,000). Total sales eased roughly 8% year-over-year as single-family inventory expanded over 20% month-over-month ahead of winter, concentrated heavily below $750,000.

Across luxury tiers, South Florida continues averaging roughly one $10 million-plus closing per day, while international buyers represent roughly half of new-construction purchases across prime [Brickell](/sell-brickell), Miami Beach, [Coral Gables](/sell-coral-gables), and West Palm Beach corridors.

## Three Late-2026 Catalysts Shifting the Sell-Now Equation

Three structural forces make late November through January distinct from prior cycles:

1. **Insurance Premium Relief vs. Condo Reserve Deadlines:** After Florida tort and market reforms reduced Citizens Property Insurance's policy count from over 1.4 million down toward 200,000 to 260,000 policies, state-approved Citizens rate reductions rolling through 2026 renewals averaged **-8.7% for HO-3 homeowners** and **-10.4% for HO-6 condo policies** (-5.9% statewide personal lines). Single-family sellers with updated 4-Point and OIR-B1-1802 wind-mitigation reports can present lower carrying costs to incoming buyers today. Conversely, qualifying condominium buildings of three or more habitable stories face a hard **December 31, 2026** statutory deadline for Milestone Inspections and non-waivable Structural Integrity Reserve Studies (SIRS) under Florida law (with HB 913 setting a $25,000 reserve-item threshold). Condo owners in compliant buildings gain an immediate credibility advantage over competing listings still unresolved in early 2027.
2. **Winter Liquidity vs. Spring Inventory Dilution:** National pending home sales fell 16.7% year-over-year in October (-4.7% month-over-month) alongside a 5.5% monthly decline in existing-home sales to 3.75 million annualized (NAR). Unlike broad Sunbelt markets with open perimeter land, South Florida sits physically bounded between the Atlantic Ocean and the Everglades Conservation Areas, supported by zero state individual income tax. When Palm Beach or Broward listings expand 20% heading into winter, early movers capture winter domestic relocation and cross-border cash capital before March and April supply crests.
3. **Constant-Quality Appreciation Beyond Short-Term Noise:** Monthly MLS medians fluctuate when the mix of luxury or entry-level closings shifts. Constant-quality Federal Housing Finance Agency (FHFA) repeat-sales indices measure appreciation on the exact same physical homes over time and should never be conflated with monthly median sale prices.
