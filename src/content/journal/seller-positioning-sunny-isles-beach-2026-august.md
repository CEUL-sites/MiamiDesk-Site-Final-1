Positioning a Sunny Isles Beach condominium before pricing it: exposure, line, and the document package, and what buyers verify first.

## What Sunny Isles Beach Buyers Are Actually Comparing

In an oceanfront tower, the line and the exposure do most of the pricing work before anyone considers the interior.

Cross-border buyers frequently evaluate the building before they evaluate the unit, which makes a complete and readable document package part of the pricing strategy rather than paperwork. The likely buyer here is drawn from international, luxury, and second-home buyers, and the property is being read against oceanfront towers where the building, the line, and the exposure carry most of the value. A list price set without knowing which comparison the property will lose is a guess wearing a number.

## The Diligence That Decides a Sunny Isles Beach Deal

The items that most often stall a sale here:

- Association reserves, assessment history, and structural inspection status
- The building's leasing policy
- How oceanfront exposure affects maintenance and insurance on the unit

None of them improve by waiting, and each is cheaper to resolve on the seller's own schedule than under a contract deadline with a buyer holding the calendar. The specific failure to plan around is a cross-border buyer stalling on documents that were never assembled in advance.

## Where the Price Actually Comes From

Pricing should be built from direct versus partial ocean exposure, line and floor within the tower, and finish level and whether furnishings convey — not from a per-square-foot figure applied across the city. Overpricing does not create negotiating room. It removes the property from the searches of the buyers most likely to pay full value, and the correction costs more than the original discipline would have.
