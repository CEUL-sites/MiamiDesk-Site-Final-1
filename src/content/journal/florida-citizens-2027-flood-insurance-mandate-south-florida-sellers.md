Effective **January 1, 2027**, Florida Statutes § 627.351(6) enters its final phase: **every remaining Citizens Property Insurance personal residential policyholder with wind coverage must carry separate flood insurance**, regardless of FEMA flood zone—including low-risk Zone X—or dwelling value. While Florida OIR-approved **2026 Citizens rate decreases** trimmed tri-county HO-3 renewals by **11.7% to 13.0%**, the incoming flood mandate and the **20% private-carrier takeout rule** directly shape buyer debt-to-income (DTI) qualification and seller net proceeds across Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach. Because NFIP policies often carry a 30-day waiting period, late November 2026 is the final planning window for January renewals and winter listings.

## The January 1, 2027 Citizens Flood Mandate Schedule

Enacted in December 2022, Florida's rollout tied mandatory flood coverage to FEMA hazard zones first and stepped down by Coverage A dwelling value until every remaining wind policy enters the mandate on January 1, 2027:

| Effective Date | Covered Citizens Policies (With Wind) | FEMA Zone Scope | Compliance Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Apr / Jul 2023** | Properties in FEMA SFHA | Zones A / AE / VE | Separate NFIP or private flood |
| **Jan 1, 2024** | Coverage A **$600,000+** | All zones (incl. Zone X) | Proof of flood + `CIT FW01` |
| **Jan 1, 2025** | Coverage A **$500,000+** | All zones (incl. Zone X) | Proof of flood + `CIT FW01` |
| **Jan 1, 2026** | Coverage A **$400,000+** | All zones (incl. Zone X) | Proof of flood + `CIT FW01` |
| **Jan 1, 2027** | **All remaining wind policies** | **All zones, any value** | **Mandatory for renewal/new** |

**Exemptions:** Condominium unit-owner policies (**HO-6**), tenant policies (**HO-4**), and policies excluding windstorm/hail remain exempt from this personal mandate. Condo buyers and sellers still face building-level master insurance and Fannie Mae warrantability checks, detailed in our [2026 South Florida condo warrantability guide](/journal/south-florida-condo-warrantability-fannie-mae-full-review-2026).

Citizens does not sell flood insurance directly. Affected owners must bind separate NFIP or private flood coverage and submit proof plus Form `CIT FW01` before renewal to avoid non-renewal notices.

## Four Pre-Listing Insurance Rules for South Florida Sellers

1. **Assemble a Pre-Listing Insurance Packet:** Pair your current declarations page with a current **4-Point Inspection**, a **Uniform Mitigation Verification Inspection (OIR-B1-1802)** documenting roof age and opening protection, and your mandatory [Florida statutory flood disclosure](/journal/florida-flood-disclosure-home-sellers-2026). See our [roof and wind-mitigation insurability guide](/journal/insurability-roof-wind-mitigation-south-florida-sale-2026) for credits that strengthen buyer quotes.
2. **Use the 20% Private-Carrier Takeout Rule:** Under Citizens depopulation rules, if an approved private carrier offers comparable coverage within **20%** of the Citizens premium, the home is generally ineligible for Citizens. Because private carriers do not impose the Zone X flood mandate on non-SFHA homes, a private quote can lower a buyer's *combined* hazard-plus-flood escrow.
3. **Prepare Sub-$400K Coverage A Homes Early:** Established single-family homes across [Plantation](/sell-plantation), [Pembroke Pines](/sell-pembroke-pines), [Sunrise](/sell-sunrise), and [Kendall](/sell-kendall) that remained exempt through 2026 enter the mandatory flood tier on January 1, 2027. Disclosing insurability upfront preserves contract certainty.
4. **Separate Condo Unit Rules From Master Policies:** While HO-6 policies are exempt from the personal Citizens flood mandate, buyers in [Brickell](/sell-brickell), [Aventura](/sell-aventura), and [Fort Lauderdale](/sell-fort-lauderdale) still underwrite association master policies and December 31, 2026 SIRS reserve compliance.
