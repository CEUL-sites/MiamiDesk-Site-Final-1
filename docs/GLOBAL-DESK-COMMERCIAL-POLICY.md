# Miami Global Desk — Commercial Policy

**Effective:** September 2026  
**Status:** Governing internal reference  
**Applies to:** New Miami Global Desk opportunities, qualification, proposals, internal recommendations, and future website or agreement work

## Commercial lanes

| Opportunity | Commercial treatment |
|---|---|
| New non-exclusive or short-term property | **EUR 200 activation per property + EUR 100 per month per property** |
| Meaningful exclusive mandate expected to run approximately six months or longer | **Bespoke Strategic Mandate Review** before proposing scope or price |
| Prior bespoke arrangement | Preserve the agreed or approved terms; do not replace them with the new default |

The standard activation and monthly fees pay for the professional distribution,
documentation, buyer-agent activation, and cooperation infrastructure. They do
not include transaction compensation.

## Transaction compensation

Transaction compensation is separate from activation and monthly service fees.
It must be property-specific, agreed in writing through the applicable
professional or brokerage parties, and confirmed before any activity that
depends on it. Do not imply that compensation is included, automatic, published
through an MLS, or guaranteed.

## Positioning standard

Describe Miami Global Desk as a professional infrastructure that can:

- prepare eligible property information and supporting documentation;
- position selected inventory for professional South Florida distribution;
- create buyer-agent visibility and cooperation pathways;
- coordinate qualified introductions with the originating professional; and
- preserve the originating mandate, client relationship, local representation,
  negotiation, and closing.

Use phrases such as **professional distribution**, **buyer-agent activation**,
**cooperation infrastructure**, **eligible platform pathways**, and **subject to
property, brokerage, platform, legal, and compliance review**.

Never describe the service as direct MLS access. Never promise automatic or
guaranteed acceptance, placement, syndication, leads, buyers, price, timeline,
or sale. Carlos acts as the Florida-licensed principal through United Realty
Group; he does not provide unlicensed representation in another jurisdiction.

## Decision sequence

1. Confirm that the opportunity is new or governed by a prior arrangement.
2. Confirm mandate type and expected duration.
3. Apply the standard per-property structure only to a new non-exclusive or
   short-term opportunity.
4. Route a meaningful approximately six-month-or-longer exclusive mandate to a
   Strategic Mandate Review.
5. Define transaction compensation separately and in writing.
6. Confirm property eligibility, distribution channels, documentation,
   cooperation roles, reporting, and compliance before activation.

## Publication and agreement control

This is an internal operating policy. Do not publish its prices on the public
website or alter agreement language unless Carlos explicitly requests that
specific output or release. Any future public copy must preserve the positioning
and claims boundaries above.
