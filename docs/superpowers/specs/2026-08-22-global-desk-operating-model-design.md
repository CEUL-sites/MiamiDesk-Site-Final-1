# Global Desk Operating Model Design

## Objective

Reposition Global Desk as a brokerage-led international property activation and cooperation service. The product is professional distribution converted into professional cooperation, not passive advertising, vague exposure, or numerical access to an association.

## Canonical product architecture

- `/global-desk` is the English canonical page.
- `/es/global-desk` is the Spanish canonical page.
- `/es/spain-desk` permanently redirects to `/es/global-desk`.
- `/spain-mls-listing` permanently redirects to `/global-desk`.
- Both canonical pages render one shared React page with route-derived language. No local-storage language toggle changes canonical content.

## Narrative

1. Hero: define the product and licensed Florida-side counterpart.
2. Audience: distinguish selected individual inventory from recurring developer/agency mandates.
3. Commercial difference: contrast passive advertising with professional activation and cooperation.
4. Mandate protection: show what the originating professional retains and what Global Desk adds.
5. Institutional proof: use 93,000 members, 437+ agreements, and 11 MLS data exchanges only as sourced credibility.
6. Qualified buyer introduction: explain inquiry, qualified introduction, and financially verified/priority introduction without using “pre-approved” absent lender evidence.
7. Agent protection: remain involved or refer and transfer; 365-day protection is subject to written agreement and brokerage approval.
8. Activation: Qualify, Prepare, Activate, Coordinate.
9. Developer/agency mandate: recurring inventory receives a broader privately documented scope.
10. Conversion: three progressive entry paths with contact details first and conditional detail second.
11. FAQ/compliance: concise non-guarantee and brokerage-approval language.

## Form behavior

Preserve Netlify Forms, direct backup notification, deduplication, lead scoring, acknowledgment email, attribution, analytics, honeypot, render-time guard, and WhatsApp routes. First interaction asks only for path, name, company, email, and phone. Detailed property/mandate/buyer fields appear after the visitor continues. Uploads remain optional and never block the first interaction.

## Visual system

Keep the existing HomesProfessional navy `#0B1E3F`, ivory `#F9F7F2`, restrained gold `#B08D57`, editorial serif, sans-serif body, cinematic real-estate media, and existing global chrome. Use open bands, rules, whitespace, and at most one strong media moment per narrative phase. Avoid repeated card grids and above-the-fold statistics.

## Compliance constraints

Follow `CLAUDE.md`. Remove `$69B` from the Global Desk. Do not guarantee MLS or portal inclusion, distribution, outreach, buyer interest, leads, offers, commissions, or sales. Do not imply Carlos is licensed outside Florida. Compensation and registration protection remain subject to applicable written agreements and brokerage approval. Every statistic displays its repository-approved source.

## Acceptance

Within about 30 seconds a first-time developer or listing professional can identify the product, commercial value, mandate benefit, qualified handoff, agent-control options, licensed structure, and next action in both languages. Desktop and mobile render cleanly; all three form paths work; canonical/hreflang/redirect/prerender/sitemap configuration agrees.
